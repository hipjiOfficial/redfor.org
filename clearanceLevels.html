Clearance levels allow for tight security and supervision within redfor agents.

The following is a list of each level of clearance, and what it grants.

In order to be viewing this, you are already One Star.

Security Clearance Levels

One Star [Agent][☆] - allows access to most redfor.org pages.

Two Star [Operative][☆☆] - given once a discord account is attached to a redfor.org account. gives access to more specialized tools such as LetMeKnow, and forums.

Three Star [Special Agent][☆☆☆]

Four Star [Special Operative][☆☆☆☆] - highest level of clearance for a regular field agent

Five Star [Senior Special Agent][☆☆☆☆☆] - beginning of specialized redfor staff level clearance. they are allowed to log the shop and have a @redfor.org email to allow for official contact.

Six Star [Administrator][☆☆☆☆☆☆] - api clearance

Six Point Five Star [Senior Administrator][☆☆☆☆☆☆/☆] - second highest clearance. if a seven star should ever become incapacitated, the six point five is in reserve to take over seven star clearance.

Seven Star [Director][☆☆☆☆☆☆☆] - only one person is allowed to have seven star clearance at a time. They oversee and is able to manipulate all redfor property.
